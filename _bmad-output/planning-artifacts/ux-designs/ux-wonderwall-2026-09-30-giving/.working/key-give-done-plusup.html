What this shows. The decided state in the same visit: a Done block (2px citron rule) with the "Given · $5" chip, then the plus-up ask toward the same target, then history. Creative and project targets get the frequency toggle, four amount buttons and one citron button whose label states amount and frequency; the hint under it is CLAIMS.patron then CLAIMS.processingFee, verbatim. The fund target gets the two Sophia Fund links (the same two links /fund/sophia offers) and CLAIMS.grantFundDeductible; that is the only place that CLAIMS sentence appears.
  • $5 is computed, never typed. It is the pool share of this member's own invoice (The Garden: $10 membership × 50% pool share = $5). A $20 membership would read $10; a community with no pool share opens nothing. Every string that shows the amount takes it from the invoice.
  • History: period · what you did · what you added. A defaulted month reads "Stayed in the grant fund. You didn't pick." with no chip and no citron.
  • "Given to you" (from getMyGiving.received and owedCents): what reached this member as a creative, with the owed amount and the Settings link while unconnected. A connected recipient's notice sends them here ("See what you've been given").
  • "Not this month" collapses the panel; abandoning checkout returns here with the panel still open.
