What this shows. The new "Get paid" panel above Billing in Settings › Money, same heading style as Billing. Four states: not connected (button opens Stripe Express, a hosted page; we render no identity or bank fields), checking (returned with ?connect=return; refreshConnectStatus runs and the panel settles), connected (citron badge, createConnectDashboardLink opens Stripe's login link in a new tab), and payouts paused (payoutsEnabled false after details were submitted; the error line under the button is the one plain error sentence). A fifth state, not finished (connect.started and not detailsSubmitted, or back on ?connect=refresh), offers to pick up where they left off. Owed and paid out are always visible once anything is owed.
  • No money sentence here beyond amounts: CLAIMS.payout describes hand payouts and is not used; the $50 transfer minimum has no CLAIMS sentence yet, so the panel does not state it (open item in EXPERIENCE.md).
  • The existing pay-handle fields (Venmo etc.) stay below Billing, unchanged.
