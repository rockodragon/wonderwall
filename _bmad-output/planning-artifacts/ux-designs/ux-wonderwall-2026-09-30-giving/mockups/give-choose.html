What this shows. The page in its open state. Heading, then the dated default line as line two, then CLAIMS.dues. Three choice cards are a radiogroup; the picker for the chosen card appears under it; the fund needs no picker. Selected cards and rows carry a citron border and a check glyph. The confirm button names the target and is disabled (50%) until one is chosen. Errors are one plain sentence under the button, body color.
  • $5 is computed, never typed. It is the pool share of this member's own invoice (The Garden: $10 membership × 50% pool share = $5). A $20 membership would read $10; a community with no pool share opens nothing. Every string that shows the amount takes it from the invoice.
  • Creative picker: search by name, 250ms debounce; empty query lists the first fifteen active members alphabetically. Self is excluded. No paid membership needed to be picked.
  • Project picker: open passion projects in the community, own projects excluded; row shows lead and raised of goal.
  • Note: 200 characters, optional. "Give anonymously" is the backing form's choice with a hint that says what the recipient sees.
