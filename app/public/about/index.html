creatives.exchange About

Nobody makes anything alone.

The Exchange is where creatives find the people who support their work — with funding, with collaboration, with a seat at a table. Sit down with people who make things. Propose the work you're called to. Get it made.

For creatives A seat, a project, and people who'll help you make it — from $10/mo. $5 funds other creatives' projects; $5 runs the place. Take a seat → For patrons Shape the creative economy of your community — cover a seat from $10/mo, fund a Fellowship, commission a project. Support a creative → For hosts You're a creative who leads a community. Get paid to gather the people you already gather. Host a table → For community partners Coffee shops, galleries, venues, libraries — you bring the place, creatives bring the people. Be the place it happens. Offer a night →

There is no algorithm here. You seek and you find — and everyone else came for the same reason you did.
