The Garden

A home for Kingdom-minded creatives — and the people who back them.

The Garden brings creative community, patronage, and public storytelling into one place. Communities gather around tables, members fund each other's projects, and patrons help shape the creative economy of their cities. Start where you fit:

For creatives Community and resources to live out your gifts — host projects, find patrons, grow with peers. → For patrons Shape the creative economy of your community — fund fellowships, projects, events, and seats. → For community leaders Cultivate a flourishing creative community — gather your people, fund the work, tell the story. →
