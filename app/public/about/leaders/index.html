The Garden
For community leaders

Cultivate a flourishing,
creative community.

You lead a community of Kingdom-minded creatives. The Garden gives you the tools to cultivate it — gather your people around tables, fund the work they're called to, and tell the stories that help the whole garden grow.

What you can cultivate

Gather your people
Profiles, portfolios, tables, and events — small groups, cohorts, and gatherings, all in one place instead of five tools.
Fund the work
Projects and fellowships — members propose, the community discerns, patrons and churches fund. Half of all membership dues flow to creative projects.
Tell the story
A public showcase page for every funded work — the proof that draws your next members and patrons in.
