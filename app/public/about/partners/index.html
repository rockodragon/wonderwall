The Garden For community partners

Be the place it happens.

A community partner is a local organization with something the scene needs — a room, a stage, a channel, the materials themselves. Coffee shops, galleries, venues, libraries, supply shops, podcasts and channels that feature local work, churches with a building. You bring what you have; creatives bring the people. Your slow Tuesday becomes their show — and their crowd becomes your regulars.

What you can offer
Space
A wall, a night, a stage. The back room on a Tuesday. The month your walls sit empty.
Goods & services
Paint, clay, film, gear, studio time — the materials of making. Ten free prints a month goes further than you'd think.
Audience & channels
Your feed, your feature slot, your foot traffic — a podcast episode, a channel spotlight, a mailing list. Put local work in front of the people who already follow you, online or in the room.
Money
Sponsor a table or a show — when you want to fund the scene directly, that door is open too.

Why partners say yes

  • People in the room on a dead night — people who come back
  • A standing place in your city's creative conversation
  • A credit you can point at — your name on every work made in your space

How it starts

  • Post one offer — a night, a wall, ten prints a month
  • No fee, no contract, no minimum — one-off is a complete relationship
  • Grow only if you want to — one-off, recurring, or anchor. Most partners never need more than step one.
