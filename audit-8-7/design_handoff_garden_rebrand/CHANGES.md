## 2. "Back" → "support"

`back` / `backed by` reads as money only. Support is funding, collaboration, and participation.

| Find | Replace |
|------|---------|
| `backed by patrons and by each other` | `supported by patrons and by each other` |
| `let the community and its patrons back it` | `let the community and its patrons support it` |
| `the people who back them` | `the people who support their work` |
| `Water the garden` (donate CTA) | `Support a creative` |
| `help set the table` | `Cover a seat` |

**Keep `back`/`funded` only where it literally means cash** — e.g. "this project is 60% funded".

## 3. Headline and framing rewrites

### Homepage hero

**Was:**
> Show and grow your craft. Collaborate & find work.
> A creative community for Kingdom-minded artists, makers, employers, sponsors, and collaborators.

**Now:**
> **Nobody makes anything alone.**
>
> [NAME] is where Kingdom-minded creatives find the people who support their work — with funding,
> with collaboration, with a seat at a table. Sit down with people who make things. Propose the
> work you're called to. Get it made.

Why: a five-noun list is the opposite of an emotion. The test every line must pass is
*does someone read this and think "I want to be part of that"?*

### The position line — add it, it doesn't exist yet

> **There is no algorithm here. You seek and you find — and everyone else came for the same
> reason you did.**

This is the product's strongest differentiator and it currently appears nowhere. Put it on the
homepage and in the "This week's buzz" surface footer.

### Descriptor — use beside the name everywhere

> Where Kingdom-minded creatives find the people who support their work.

### Tagline — one, everywhere, including the patrons page

Currently the creatives and leaders pages close with "A home for Kingdom-minded creatives" while
the **patrons** page closes with "For the flourishing of the arts" — the one page in front of
people writing large cheques is the one that drops the faith frame. Pick one and use it on all
three. Recommendation: keep the faith frame; it's the moat, not the liability.

### Host page

**Was:** "You lead a community of Kingdom-minded creatives."
**Now:** **"You're a Kingdom-minded creative who leads a community."**

Puts them inside the community rather than above it — a practitioner, not staff, which is the
reason people follow them.

**Was:** "Gather your people"
**Now:** **"Gather with like-minded people"** — and for creatives, **"Find the rooms you want to
be in."**

"Your people" assumes you already have them and casts them as yours to manage. *With* is
peer-level — an invitation rather than an admin task.

**Add, now that hosts can earn:**
> Get paid to gather the people you already gather.

Compare to "$50/mo for community tools" — one is a cost centre, the other is a side income for
someone already doing the work for free. This is the biggest single copy upgrade available.

### Creatives page

**Was:** "Community and resources to live out your gifts."
**Now:** **"A seat, a project, and people who'll help you make it."**

**Was:** "Become a Guide — $50/mo"
**Now:** **"Host a table — $50/mo"** (and see Task 5 — this price may change)

### Patrons page — add the small door

The smallest current ask is a $500/mo Fellowship. Add above it:

> **Cover one seat — $10/mo.** Pay for one creative's place at a table. You'll get their story
> updates, with your name in the credit line.

Nobody's first patron action should be $500.

### Remove from the homepage hero

**"Closed Beta · Invite Only"** — it's the first thing a creative reads and it means *not for you
yet*. Move it into the invite field as a label.

## 4. Banned words — add to a copy review checklist

**Metaphor budget is two: Garden and Table.** The third metaphor is what kills it. Currently the
copy also runs board, fellowship-as-image, pathfinder, seed, and journey.

Banned:

- Seeds, soil, roots, watering, harvest **as verbs or CTAs** ("Water the garden")
- Pathways, journeys, bridges, ecosystems, unlocking
- `platform`, `marketplace`, `solution` — say what happens instead: someone funds someone's work
- `trending`, `popular`, `top`, `featured` **by metric** — violates the no-algorithm position
- Any per-person count in copy: followers, views, likes

Allowed and encouraged: *table, seat, host, room, house show, credit, supported by.*

Note: "buzz" is allowed — bees live in gardens.

## 6. The 50% line — promote it

> Half of every membership funds another creative's project. From day one your money is
> supporting someone — instead of hoping to hear back.

This is the sentence that makes the product an *economy* rather than a subscription. It is
currently buried mid-page on one of three routes. It belongs on the homepage, above the fold.

**And:** if host fees don't feed the pool, add one clarifying sentence wherever this appears.
