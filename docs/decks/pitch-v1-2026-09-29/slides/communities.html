Who it is for

Real gatherings that already exist, with people in them now.

The Garden

The Christian creative community in San Diego, and where this started. Anyone can sit at an open table; you don't have to be a Christian to show work, get feedback, or get paid. Run by the company with Abiding Practice and The Table Art Society.

Abiding Practice · The Table Art Society

Founding communities. Spiritual formation for artists through film, podcast and retreats; a nine-month artist cohort in La Mesa. Both already run classes, showcases and gatherings on group chats and spreadsheets.

Churches and sponsors

Buy seats for their people and hand out codes; give to a fund through the 501(c)(3); post paid work with the pay stated up front. Ten seats for a year is one $1,000 charge.

Civic and neighborhood communities

The same three blocks with different words: a downtown creative-workforce group, a North County artist alliance, a city's creative youth program. The platform is open to any creative.

What we ask of a founding community: a real gathering, one named person who runs the room, honesty about what we replace versus complement

The civic card is aspiration, not a signed partner. Say so if asked.
