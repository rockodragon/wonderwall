What the money does

Every 100 members put $500 a month into grants for other creatives.

Kind of grantTypical sizeWhat it pays for

creatives reached in a year when the fund takes in $5,000 a month: three fellowships, twelve project grants, six commissions, twenty covered seats, two dozen micro-grants.
