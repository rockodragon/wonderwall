How it works

Three layers. The community is what people join. The platform runs underneath.

1 · Communities

Run by their own people

The Garden (Christian creatives, San Diego), Abiding Practice, The Table Art Society. Next: churches buying seats, civic and neighborhood groups. Each sets its own values, house rules, and price.

2 · The platform

One account, every community

Profiles and portfolios, projects, paid gigs, events, classes, covered seats, backing checkout, fund pages, payouts. Rules enforced on the server. No algorithmic feed, no follower counts.

3 · Money to creatives

Backings, classes, grants, jobs

A patron backs a named person or project and 90% goes to them. A host keeps 90% of a class. Half of every membership funds grants. Paid work is posted with the pay stated up front.

Same three blocks on every community page: who this is for, what we believe, house rules. Only the words change. That is how a Christian community and a civic one stand on the same ground.
