The business

We earn when creatives get paid. About $5 of platform revenue per member per month across the mix.

Revenue lines

  • 10% of backings and class sales; 5% on any part of a gift over $1,000
  • $10 memberships, half of which fund grants; churches buy seats in bulk
  • 5% platform fee on gifts to a partner-administered grant fund
  • Sponsorships from businesses that want their name on the work
  • Later: a host tier for running funding programs; two more metros
