The problem

Creative work gets found in a group chat and paid for out of goodwill.

10%

is what a creative pays Substack or Patreon on every dollar, forever, running a community on three or four tools taped together.

85%

is the cut San Diego's mayor proposed to city arts grants in April 2026, from $13.8M to about $2M, before a coalition put $10.35M back.

$1.2B

of economic activity and about 17,000 jobs from San Diego's arts nonprofits in 2022, while the region's creative workforce shrank in the Otis College data.

The institutions get the grants. The people who make the work get exposure, a Venmo handle, and a fee on the way out. Nobody moves money to named creative work, month after month, in a way a patron, a church or a city can see.

Sources: Substack and Patreon pricing pages · KPBS, June 2026 · San Diego Magazine Fall Arts Preview 2026 (AEP6) · Otis College Report on the Creative Economy, 2025

Three numbers, all verified in docs/civic-partners-social-graph.md section 11. Do not add the SF or LA comparisons; those are not verified.
