The honest questions

What could make this fail, and what we are doing about it.

The questionOur answer
Will creatives pay and renew when they never receive a grant?You can always show up free. You pay when you want your work funded, so we bring patrons and partners first and members second. Year one is partner-funded in every scenario.
Will churches and patrons put real money in?Ten covered seats is one $1,000 charge. Backing a named person on your phone in the room is the Nov 6 test. Two or three committed churches fund a $5,000-a-month grant fund.
Is this a ministry with a platform attached?The platform is open to any creative. The Garden is the Christian community on it and where it started. Purpose: human creativity in service of human flourishing. House rules are civic.
Is the company skimming donations?Business money and charity money never mix. The 501(c)(3) partner receives gifts, issues receipts and decides grants; it keeps 5% and the platform 5%. Backing is never called a donation.
Payments, payouts, complianceStripe processes and pays out; no customer funds held; $50 payout minimum; 1099s at $600; no chance-based mechanics. Payout speed is never promised.
One founder, unpaidThe largest line in the budget is the one nobody invoices. The raise funds the first community hire and a partial founder salary, in that order.
