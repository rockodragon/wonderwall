What it is

creatives.exchange is where creatives find paid work, get backed by people who believe in them, and apply for grants.

Communities run on top of it. People hold one account and belong to many communities.

We earn 10% of the money that moves. Hosting is free. Joining is free.

Grants run through a 501(c)(3) partner, so gifts are tax-deductible and every grant a fund makes is listed.
