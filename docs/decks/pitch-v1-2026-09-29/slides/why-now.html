Why San Diego, why now

The region just proved it will move money to keep creative work alive. What is missing is the rail.

$10.35M

restored to city arts funding in June 2026 by Council, County and the Prebys Foundation, including $3M direct from Prebys

$1.3M

going directly to 42 working creatives through Artists Count, a binational fund and census launched in April 2026

4

state-designated cultural districts in the county, twice as many as Los Angeles County or San Francisco

$6.3M

paid by Prebys for the California Theatre site to unblock a downtown Art + Design District built for the creative workforce, not tourists

Institutions receive the money and creatives receive exposure. Skool moves content and payments; Substack and Patreon take 10% and keep the audience; Kickstarter funds a campaign and leaves. We haven't found a platform that moves money to named creatives inside real communities, month after month.

San Diego's arts leadership is asking for exactly this: a district that connects anchor institutions to working creatives, affordability and space for creative workers, and money that reaches the people who make the work. That is what a rail is for.
