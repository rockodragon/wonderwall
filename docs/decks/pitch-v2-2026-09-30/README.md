## What the revision changed

- Brand: theCreative.exchange, the site's mark and wordmark, the tagline "Give. Receive. Grow.", citron #d7f25a as an accent only. No "made by humans" line anywhere.
- Problem: a human picture (three jobs, posting into the void, alone, not worthy) and the consequence for the economy. Not institutions.
- Solution: creative communities that support themselves and step up to their opportunities; the financial, social and emotional, and cultural outcomes sit above the product.
- Revenue model: a Sankey of the Base-case year-five mix (memberships and seat packs, project backings, classes and content, gifts and sponsorships) into creatives, communities, the fellowship program and the platform.
- Who it serves: theCreative.exchange at the center; creatives, communities, businesses, churches, universities, patrons, cultural districts and the city, fellowship programs as constituents.
- Market: a plain TAM / SAM / SOM ladder. Business: one stacked bar chart and three lines. Ask: half the text; impact report every six months.
- Where it stands: soft-launched September 28 (30 creatives on the waitlist), two communities live with The Table Art Society, The Grove, Abiding Practice and Temple; kickoff in the Christian arts community November 6.
- The people: Rick Moy, Haley Dall, Patricia Sinay.
