# Pitch deck v2, 2026-09-30

Live deck: https://claude.ai/artifact/LNyg8n2CZTRUEuJbjz8CWn (private until shared). Deck v1 (16 slides, 2026-09-29) is preserved untouched at https://claude.ai/artifact/VVTMpZMN4cz6vyYkx5ksyo and in `../pitch-v1-2026-09-29/`.

These are the slide sources (`deck.json` plus one HTML file per slide), saved so the deck can be rebuilt or edited outside the artifact.

## What changed from v1

- 12 slides instead of 16, in the standard pitch order.
- Four diagrams: the two circuits of the status quo, the three-layer shift, the money-flow diagram (five ways in, four ways out), the community map, and the market rings.
- Supporting facts cut to one line per slide; each claim is written to land and invite a question. The speaker notes (the `<aside>` in each file) hold the numbers to answer with.
- The nonprofit side is a managed program run by a 501(c)(3) partner, never a regranting pool.
- Impact language: flourishing, belonging, diversity, dignity of work, agency. One slide states what changes for the creative class; one states the impact of the company and the metrics that validate it.
- Numbers come from `docs/financial-model-5yr-v2.xlsx` (Base case) and `docs/funding/market-sizing.md`.
