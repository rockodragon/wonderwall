Post-money SAFEs · Delaware C-corp formed before the first check · rolling closes to $350k · seed at 18 months

The round is $350,000 on post-money SAFEs with one valuation cap for everyone, raised in stages because pre-seed money comes in $10k to $50k angel checks: a first close of $150k targeted around November 6, then rolling closes to $350k by the end of March. The company is being formed as a Delaware C-corp before the first check (there is no entity today; the work has run through a founder's S-corp), which is what makes the SAFE available and starts the qualified-small-business-stock clock for founders and investors; a convertible note only for a lender who needs a maturity date. Base case, first 18 months: spend $396k, revenue $103k, net need $293k; $350k gives 21 months of runway, $250k gives 16. Founders are paid from the month after the first close on a ramp that steps on milestones ($4k a month each to start), and both are full time before any employee. The first employee, a community and partnerships lead, starts at month 18. The year-three proof points (30 communities, 3,000+ paying individuals) sit in a second round: a $1M to $1.2M seed at 18 months on 1,100 paying individuals, 12 communities, 90% net revenue retention and 200 organization seats. Praxis ($100k on a SAFE, spring cohort) and a foundation PRI loan are additive to the $350k, not part of it. Program grants go to our 501(c)(3) partner to fund a program it manages itself; we do not pass grant money on to other funders.
