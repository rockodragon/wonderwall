Post-money SAFEs · Delaware C-corp formed before the first check · rolling closes to $350k · seed at 18 months

The round is $350,000 on post-money SAFEs with one valuation cap for everyone (set with counsel in the $5M to $8M range; Carta's median for rounds this size is about $10M on a tech-heavy sample), raised in stages because pre-seed money comes in $10k to $50k angel checks: a first close of $150k targeted around November 6 from patrons and the founders' networks, then rolling closes to $350k by the end of March, including a Regulation Crowdfunding community round on Wefunder for members and patrons (Form C filed in December on the November numbers). Local angel groups fund biotech and software on 10x odds and are not in the plan. The company is being formed as a Delaware C-corp before the first check (there is no entity today; the work has run through a founder's S-corp), which is what makes the SAFE available and starts the qualified-small-business-stock clock for founders and investors; a convertible note only for a lender who needs a maturity date. Base case, first 18 months: spend $458k, revenue $109k, net need $348k; $350k gives 18 months of runway, $250k gives 14. The stepping stones are the round: a first close, rolling closes, then the seed. November 6 itself is five weeks out and is paid from current cash and sponsorships, not from the round; the round funds the launches after it. Founders are paid from the month after the first close on a ramp that steps on milestones ($4k a month each to start), and both are full time before any full-time employee; part-time marketing and community help starts in month 1. The first full-time employee, a community and partnerships lead, starts at month 18, and the team stays at four because franchise metros are led by their own people. The year-three proof points (55 communities, six metros, 5,000+ paying individuals) sit in a second round: a seed at 18 months, about $500k on the Base plan or $1M and up to run the faster metro rollout, on about 1,000 paying individuals, 12 communities, two metros live, 90% net revenue retention and 300 organization seats. Kiva, an Accessity loan in year two and any Prebys program money to a nonprofit partner are additive to the $350k, not part of it; a foundation PRI loan is a year-two conversation; Praxis is a 2027 application at $50k and is not counted. Program grants go to our 501(c)(3) partner to fund a program it manages itself; we do not pass grant money on to other funders.
