What changes for the creative class

Flourishing means three things you can measure in a person's life.

Financial

Dignity of work

Paid work with the pay stated up front. Backings where 90% reaches them. Fellowships that buy time, not just materials.

Social

Belonging

A table where they are known, in person and online. A seat someone can cover. Communities led by their own people, so the room looks like the city.

Cultural

Agency

They own the portfolio, the audience and the work. Stories told by the people who lived them. Made by humans, for humans, and it says so.

The accent statement slide. Financial, social, cultural: the three outcomes in the goal sentence, each with the word an impact investor listens for: dignity, belonging, agency. Diversity is built in: communities are led by their own people.
