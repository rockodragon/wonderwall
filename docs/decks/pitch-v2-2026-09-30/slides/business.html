Exchange track (partner communities, organizations and franchise metros): $10k, $75k, $304k, $801k, $1.73M. The Exchange is the company; the Garden proves the model and funds the fellowship program. Franchise metros: 2, 6, 12 and 20 active from year two, each a cluster of two to six communities led by local people; a metro costs about $12k to launch and $6k a year to support, both variable inputs in the model. Money moved through the platform: $154k, $768k, $2.85M, $7.18M, $15.3M. Reaching creatives, communities and programs: $81k, $434k, $1.66M, $4.36M, $9.62M.
