We are building the infrastructure that makes the creative economy work for creatives and the community.

A flourishing creative economy, where individual creatives improve their financial, social and cultural outcomes.

Rick Moy & Haley Dall · Founders · San Diego · 2026

One sentence is the whole deck: we are building the infrastructure that enables a flourishing creative economy, so that individual creatives improve their financial, social and cultural outcomes. The goal is to change the status quo of arts and culture. We start from the individual creative and the community around them, and we build with the institutions that fund and present the arts. The Creative Exchange is a for-profit infrastructure company, converting to a public benefit corporation, and its fellowship programs are run by our 501(c)(3) partner, Abiding Practice.
