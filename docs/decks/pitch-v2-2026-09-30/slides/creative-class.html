Impact on the creative class

Flourishing is three things you can measure in a person's life.

Financial

Greater opportunities and dignity of work

Paid work with the pay stated up front. Project backings where 90% reaches them. Fellowships that buy time.

Social and emotional

Belonging

A community that knows your name, in person and online. A seat someone can cover. Communities led by their own people, so the room looks like the city.

Cultural

Agency

They own the portfolio, the audience and the work. Their work shows up in the city.

These three are the goal statement's outcomes: better financial, social and cultural outcomes for individual creatives. Each carries the word an impact investor listens for: greater opportunities and dignity of work, belonging, agency. Diversity is built in because communities are led by their own people, so the room looks like the city. The impact slide shows how we count each one.
