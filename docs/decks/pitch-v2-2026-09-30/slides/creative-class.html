Impact on the creative class

Flourishing is three things you can measure in a person's life.

Financial

Greater opportunities and dignity of work

Paid work with the pay stated up front. Backings where 90% reaches them. Fellowships that buy time.

Social and emotional

Belonging

A community that knows your name, in person and online. Rooms led by their own people, so the room looks like the city.

Cultural

Agency

They own the portfolio, the audience and the work, and it shows up in the city: stages, walls, screens, places of work.

These three are the goal statement's outcomes: better financial, social and cultural outcomes for individual creatives. Each carries the word an impact investor listens for: greater opportunities and dignity of work, belonging, agency. The illustrations are from the UpSight visual framework set: growth in stages for the financial outcome, a campfire for belonging, a lighthouse for agency (work that is seen from a distance). Diversity is built in because communities are led by their own people, so the room looks like the city. The impact slide shows how we count each one.
