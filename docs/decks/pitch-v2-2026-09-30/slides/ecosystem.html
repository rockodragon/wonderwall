Better circulation of money: community wealth that starts with creatives, the people.

The aim is a better circulation of money for a thriving creative society: co-creating new ways of exchanging and growing community wealth that starts with creatives, the people. The Creative Exchange sits in the middle; everyone around it is a constituent, not a charity. Live today: creatives, four partner organizations (The Table Art Society, The Grove, Abiding Practice, Temple), faith institutions buying seats, and patrons backing projects. Patrons, sponsors and funders means individuals, businesses, foundations and nonprofits: a foundation funds a grant fund run by one of our 501(c)(3) partners, a business sponsors a gathering, an individual backs a named creative. Next: businesses (restaurants, bars, venues and employers), universities and schools, and cultural districts and the city. Grant funds are run by our 501(c)(3) partners. Communities include writers guilds, film clubs and arts societies as well as our partner organizations. Faith institutions and others may open their facilities and promote to their constituents. Each constituent pays for what it gets (seats, plans, sponsorships, backing fees) and sees where the money went in the six-month impact report.
