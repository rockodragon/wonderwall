Categories follow the Prebys Foundation / Upstart Co-Lab impact framework · every number comes from the platform's own records · reported every six months

Quarterly numbers cost us nothing extra because they come straight from the platform's own records: dollars paid, creatives paid, seats used, gatherings held, retention. The six-month report is the narrative on top of those numbers: what they mean, what we learned, what we will change. The local economy row is the community impact: creatives coming and doing things that grow business activity in restaurants, bars, parks and cultural districts. Businesses, churches and universities are constituents who buy seats and hire creatives, not charities. Categories follow the Prebys Foundation / Upstart Co-Lab impact framework. The second metro is a milestone we would report against, not a count.
