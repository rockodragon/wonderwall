Preliminary model. The 10% income target applies to creatives who earn through the exchange, measured by a before-and-after survey; it is not a claim about every member, and it is not something we promise a person. The model puts about $330 to $380 per active member per year through the platform to creatives, hosts and programs, well under 1% of a full-time creative income (about $58,000 to $63,000), so a blanket 10% would not be credible. these are targets, not results. The 1,000 is the Base-case count of paying individuals about 18 months in: 310 at the end of year one, 1,480 at the end of year two, 5,120 at the end of year three, so roughly 3x a year. It counts paying individuals in the house community and partner communities, not seats organizations buy. 90% net revenue retention is a target, in line with strong paid communities. The belonging measure is a short survey at signup and again later, asking members to rate belonging before and after, so we can report one common statistic. There are no diversity numbers yet: the first report sets the baseline by discipline and neighborhood. The local economy measure is a business-owner survey plus dollars transacted with local businesses through the exchange, also baselined first. Every number except the surveys comes from the platform's own records. Categories follow the Prebys Foundation / Upstart Co-Lab impact framework. We report every six months.
