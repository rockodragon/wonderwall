Preliminary model: these are targets, not results. The 1,100 is the Base-case average of paying individuals during the second year (about 18 months in): 310 in year one, 1,120 in year two, 3,200 in year three, so roughly 3x a year. It counts paying individuals in the house community and partner communities, not seats organizations buy. 90% net revenue retention is a target, in line with strong paid communities. The belonging measure is a short survey at signup and again later, asking members to rate belonging before and after, so we can report one common statistic. There are no diversity numbers yet: the first report sets the baseline by discipline and neighborhood. The local economy measure is a business-owner survey plus dollars transacted with local businesses through the exchange, also baselined first. Every number except the surveys comes from the platform's own records. Categories follow the Prebys Foundation / Upstart Co-Lab impact framework. We report every six months.
