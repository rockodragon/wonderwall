TAM: 4.3 to 5.2 million people. The US has 3.8 million paid creatives and cultural workers (NEA 2024) plus an estimated 0.5 to 1.4 million church creative, worship and media volunteers across about 373,000 US congregations. SAM, the franchise footprint: San Diego plus nine metros where the same model runs as a community led by its own people on our infrastructure. BLS counts about 450,000 payroll jobs in arts, design, entertainment and media across the ten (May 2023 metro tables: Los Angeles 201,640; Dallas–Fort Worth 44,720; Atlanta 41,040; Seattle 35,900; Phoenix 27,600; Denver 23,620; San Diego 21,760; Austin 19,600; Las Vegas 17,720; Nashville 17,040), and BLS leaves out the self-employed, who are about a third of artists (NEA), so about 675,000 creatives in all; the 2025 metro tables were not retrievable when this was built and Los Angeles has slipped since 2023, so treat it as a rounded figure. Each of those metros also has thousands of congregations (ARDA 2020: Los Angeles about 7,100, Nashville 2,700, San Diego 1,750, Las Vegas 950). Beachhead: San Diego, about 35,000: 22,000 to 30,000 working creatives (BLS payroll grossed up for the self-employed; the City's 2024 creative-economy report counts 82,000 direct creative-economy jobs on a broader definition), about 4,000 church creatives, and about 9,200 members of the national Christian creative networks we have mapped, less overlap. SOM: 5,000 to 10,000 members in three years, of which at least 3,000 are paying individuals; both are founder estimates. The individual-conversion benchmark supports only about 1,000 for San Diego alone, so 3,000 individuals needs the franchise metros: the Base case has 30 partner communities by year three and reaches 3,200 paying individuals; the Fundable case adds three metros by year four. Organization-bought seats are about 20% of year-three revenue. Sources: BLS OEWS May 2023 metro tables, NEA 2024, ARDA 2020, docs/funding/market-sizing.md.
