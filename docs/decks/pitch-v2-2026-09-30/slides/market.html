TAM: 4.3 to 5.2 million people. The US has 3.8 million paid creatives and cultural workers (NEA 2024, primary occupation: 2.7 million in creative occupations plus 1.1 million other cultural workers), plus an estimated 0.5 to 1.4 million church creative, worship and media volunteers across about 373,000 US congregations (Hartford Institute; our estimate, no published count). SAM: 26,000 to 45,000, central about 35,000. San Diego County working creatives 22,000 to 30,000, central 26,000 (BLS May 2025: 19,990 payroll jobs in arts, design, entertainment and media, grossed up for the self-employed), plus about 4,000 San Diego church creatives (1,555 congregations in the county, ARDA 2020), plus about 9,200 members of the national Christian creative networks we have mapped, less overlap. SOM: 5,000 to 10,000 members in three years. That is the founder estimate and it is not yet sourced. It counts about 1,000 paying individuals (benchmark free-to-paid conversion of 1 to 5 percent on the SAM, central 1,000, about $120k a year in individual seats; the median paid newsletter converts 0.6 percent, the top quartile 2 to 5 percent) plus seats bought by schools, universities, churches and other organizations. Ten organizations at thirty seats is 300 members from ten conversations, so schools and organizations are how the count gets past the individual ceiling. In the model the Base case reaches 5,700 paying and covered members in year three, Conservative (individuals only) 1,270 and Fundable 12,700. To source it, count San Diego universities, school districts, churches and organizations and multiply by a realistic seat-pack size. Sources: NEA 2024, BLS May 2025, ARDA 2020, docs/funding/market-sizing.md.
