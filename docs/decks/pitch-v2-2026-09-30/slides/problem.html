Two stacks of pain, one on each side, and nothing between them. Creatives: three jobs (the creative work is the one that does not pay, so it gets the leftover hours), posting into the void, alone, and not worthy, the story they start to believe. The emotional cost and the financial cost are the same cost. Businesses and organizations: they cannot find local talent they can trust, they have empty rooms and slow nights, sponsors cannot see where the money went, and the places feel generic. Each pain on the left has a counterpart on the right, and the platform is the bridge. This slide is about people and local businesses, not about institutions and not about grant budgets.
