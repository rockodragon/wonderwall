theCreative.exchange

10% fee, plans, sponsors

Gifts to the fellowship program go to our 501(c)(3) partner and are reported separately from company revenue.

Band widths are the Base-case year-five mix (about $9.3M moving through the platform), so they show where the money goes, not a promise for every transaction. Take rates: 10% on project backings, 10% on classes and content, 10% on community memberships (the community keeps 90%), 5% on event tickets (proposed). The house community's memberships fund the fellowship program by half: a $10 membership sends $5 to the program. Gifts to the fellowship program: 95% goes to the program, of which about 90% reaches creatives; 5% is platform administration and 5% is nonprofit administration. Sponsorships and plans (community and partner plans, proposed) are 100% company revenue. The platform node is about 16% of the picture, not 10%, because on top of the 10% fee it carries sponsorships, plans and the house community's half of its memberships. Creatives get about 57%, communities 19%, the fellowship program 8%. The program is run by our 501(c)(3) partner, Abiding Practice; gifts are reported separately from company revenue.
