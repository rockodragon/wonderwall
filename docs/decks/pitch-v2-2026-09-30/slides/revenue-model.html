Band widths are the Base-case year-five mix, so they show where the money goes, not a promise for every transaction. Take rates: 10% on project backings, 10% on classes and content, 10% on community memberships (the community keeps 90%). The house community's memberships fund the fellowship program by half: a $10 membership sends $5 to the program. Gifts to the fellowship program: about 90% reaches creatives, 5% is platform administration, 5% is nonprofit administration. Sponsorships and plans (seat packs, community and partner plans) are 100% company revenue. The platform node is about 13% of the picture, not 10%, because it also carries sponsorships, plans and the house community's half of its memberships on top of the take rate. The program is run by our 501(c)(3) partner, Abiding Practice; gifts are reported separately from company revenue. Events (5% ticket fee, proposed) are left out to keep the picture simple.
