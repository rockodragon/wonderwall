Revenue model

Multiple commerce streams.

Memberships & seat packs

$10 a month; schools and organizations buy packs

Project backings

patrons back a specific project

Classes, content & events

paid by the people who take part

Gifts, sponsors & plans

Creatives

90% of backings, classes and events

Communities

90% of their memberships

Grant funds

half of house memberships, most gifts

theCreative.exchange

10% fee, plans, sponsors

Gifts to grant funds go to our 501(c)(3) partners and are reported separately from company revenue.

Band widths are the Base-case year-five mix (about $15.3M moving through the platform), so they show where the money goes, not a promise for every transaction. Take rates: 10% on project backings, 10% on classes and content, 10% on community memberships (the community keeps 90%), 5% on event tickets (proposed). Half of each house-community membership goes to grant funds: a $10 membership sends $5 to the fund. Gifts to grant funds: 95% goes to the fund, of which about 90% reaches creatives; 5% is platform administration and 5% is nonprofit administration. Sponsorships and plans (community and partner plans, proposed) are 100% company revenue. The platform node is about 14% of the picture, not 10%, because on top of the 10% fee it carries sponsorships, plans and the house community's half of its memberships. Creatives get about 60%, communities 21%, grant funds 5%. Grant funds are run by our 501(c)(3) partners; gifts are reported separately from company revenue.
