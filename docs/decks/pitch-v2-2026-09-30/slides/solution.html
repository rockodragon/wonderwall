Read the slide top to bottom, in order of importance. The top row is the point: financial outcomes (paid work, project backings, fellowships, greater opportunities), social and emotional outcomes (belonging, a community that knows your name, peers) and cultural outcomes (work that shows up in the city, in stages, walls, screens and places of work). Communities are the middle row: they are run by their own people, and people join a community, not a platform. The product is last and smallest on purpose. It exists to make the top row true. What is live today: profiles, projects, paid work listings, events with public RSVP, communities and classes, covered seats, program pages, and backing checkout on Stripe. Membership is $10 a month and half of it funds the fellowship program, which is run by our 501(c)(3) partner. Soft launch was September 28, 2026; 30 creatives are enrolled on the waitlist and two communities are live. Partner organizations on the platform include The Table Art Society, The Grove, Abiding Practice and Temple. Churches and organizations can buy seats in packs.
