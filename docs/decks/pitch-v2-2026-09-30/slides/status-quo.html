The status quo

Institutions receive the money. Creatives receive exposure.

Foundations and cities

grant to organizations

Institutions

museums, theatres, festivals

Programs and stages

creatives take part, unpaid

Patrons, churches, neighbors

want to back a person they know

Group chats and goodwill

a Venmo handle, a 10% fee on the way out

The creative

who made the work

Two circuits that never touch. The top one is funded and counted. The bottom one is where the work, the belonging and the livelihoods actually happen.

In 2026 San Diego nearly cut city arts grants by 85%; a coalition put $10.35M back. Almost none of it reaches an individual creative.

Two circuits: institutional money on top, human money on the bottom, and nothing connecting them. Invite the question: how much of the $10.35M reaches a person who makes things? We think very little, and we can't prove it, which is the point.
