Use of funds, Base case, months 1 to 18, from the model's Use of Funds sheet, shown by what the money buys rather than by department: founders $204k (47%), both full time from the first close on the pay ramp ($4k a month each to start, stepping up on milestones); marketing and launch $97k (22%), which includes a part-time launch and content helper from month 1 (a quarter of a full-time equivalent, half from year two) plus November 6, the film series, content and outreach; programs and community $66k (15%), which is gathering stipends and venues, the part-time coordinator, fellowship program administration and the community and partnerships lead at half time from month 18; operations $35k (8%); engineering and platform $25k (6%), hosting, contractors and payments compliance, because Rick is the engineering team; payments $7k; support $5k. Total $438k against $103k of revenue, net need $335k, so $350k is 19 months of runway with about $15k of cushion. Marketing is more than engineering on purpose: the product is built and the next eighteen months are launches. If the round comes in light, hold the founder pay at its current step first and move the community lead a quarter; never November 6.
