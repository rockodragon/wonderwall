# Christian arts funding: who funds it, and what they can fund for us

2026-09-30. Companion to the [funding strategy](funding-strategy.md). Checked against the funders' own pages on this date; ⚠ marks a fact from a search snippet where the page would not load. Sizes are "not published" where the funder does not publish them; no guesses.

## The bottom line

There is one open, arts-specific Christian grant program in the country with real money and a date: the Lilly-funded **Creative Arts Collective** at Belmont University, letter of intent due November 2. There is one small, fast one: **Calvin's Vital Worship grants**, due October 15. Everything else in classic Christian philanthropy is invitation-only, nonprofit-only, regional, or excludes the arts. For the for-profit, the Christian money comes through two doors: individual believers writing SAFEs in the room, and donors who move donor-advised-fund money into the company through **Impact Foundation**. There is no faith-driven angel group in Southern California, and the faith-driven networks that do invest are post-revenue.

## 2. Christian money into the for-profit

| Door | How it works | Cost and catch |
|---|---|---|
| **Believers in the room, on SAFEs** | the same first close as the strategy doc: patrons of the communities, church members with money | nothing new; this is most of the Christian money we will ever raise |
| **Impact Foundation** (Kansas): a donor's DAF or foundation money into a private company | the company applies, a committee reviews in 7 to 10 working days, the donor signs a recommendation letter, Impact wires the funds; debt, equity or a blend | 3% origination (minimum $1,000, maximum $90,000), then 0.85% a year on balances under $3M; SAFEs are not named on the site, confirm; returns go back to the donor's DAF for future giving, not to the donor, so the pitch to that donor is impact, not return; whether NCF donors can use it is not stated by either side, confirm |
| **National Christian Foundation** (NCF) | the largest Christian DAF sponsor ($987M granted in 2025); California office in Costa Mesa, no San Diego office confirmed | for the nonprofit partners: get both approved as NCF grantees before November 6 so a donor in the room can give from a DAF that night. NCF's own impact-investing pools start at $100k, giver-recommended around $250k: too large for us |
| **WaterStone** | a Christian DAF sponsor with a faith-led tech fund (with the Tebow Group and Caplin Ventures) ⚠ | stage and minimums unverified |
| **Ambassadors Impact Network** | accredited faith-driven angels; $27M into 60+ companies; pooled checks of $250k to $500k; minimum raise $200k | post-revenue only; year two |
| **Lion's Den DFW** | an annual faith-driven pitch competition (11th in April 2026, ten finalists) | stage, revenue and prize terms not published; visibility, not a plan |
| **Beyond Angels** | early stage with traction; $800k into two companies in 2021 | check sizes not published; domain now points to a sale page |
| **Sovereign's Capital, Kingdom Capital, Eventide** | seed and Series A technology, and crossover biotech | not a match for this return profile |

## 3. Donor channels, not sources

NCF, The Signatry, WaterStone and Barnabas Foundation are DAF sponsors: a believer who already has an account can grant to the nonprofit partners in minutes, and, through Impact Foundation, invest in the company. Locally, the Rancho Santa Fe Foundation (secular; $19.8M granted in 2025, awards from $5,250 to $750,000 ⚠) is where Encinitas and Rancho Santa Fe donors hold DAFs; the San Diego Christian Foundation exists but granted about $65k in 2024 ⚠. GiveSendGo is a campaign tool (0% platform fee, processing plus a tip ⚠), not capital. Church of the City in New York commissioned ten artists, a patronage model a partner church could copy; amounts not published.

## What to do, in order

1. **October 15:** Abiding Practice applies to Calvin's Vital Worship grants for a worship-arts project in The Garden ($8k to $25k). Two weeks; Haley writes it.
2. **October 1 to November 2:** the partner best placed (The Table Art Society if it is the secular arts nonprofit with its own board; otherwise Abiding Practice) files the Creative Arts Collective LOI for an Artist in Context grant ($50k to $80k a year) for its fellowship or cohort program. Ask the program staff before the LOI whether a fiscally sponsored program qualifies and whether paying a founder-affiliated platform at list price is acceptable; disclose it either way.
3. **Before November 6:** get both nonprofits approved as NCF grantees; ask Impact Foundation whether it will hold a post-money SAFE and whether NCF donors can direct money through it. Then the ask in the room has three answers: write a check, give from your DAF to the program, or invest from your DAF in the company.
4. **Warm introductions, no deadline:** the Ahmansons (Fieldstead), Rancho Santa Fe Foundation DAF holders in Encinitas, and any church in the network that wants to copy the Church of the City commissioning model.
5. **Year two:** Ambassadors Impact Network once there is revenue; Lion's Den for visibility if the 2027 dates fit.
