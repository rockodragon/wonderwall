# Patron video — brief

v1 · 2026-10-01 · separate from the welcome how-to  
Length target: 60–90 seconds  
Ends on: **Back this.**

## Job

One real creative. What they're making. How backing works, in one line. Make a stranger want to back that work.

This is not the welcome tour. No join flow, no monthly grant, no grant committee.

## One-line spine

[Name] is making [work]. Back this. You keep 90% of what a backer gives you.

**Claim for the money line:** `CLAIMS.backingShort`  
**Fee, if needed once:** `CLAIMS.processingFee` (info, not the hook)

## Suggested beats (60–90s)

| Time | Visual | Voice / caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Creative at work / the piece | Name. What they're making. One concrete detail. |
| 0:15–0:35 | Project page, goal if any | Why it matters in one sentence. No promise it will get funded. |
| 0:35–0:55 | Back this → amount (test/cutaway) | You keep 90% of what a backer gives you. |
| 0:55–1:10 | Named or anonymous choice | You can be named on the work, or stay anonymous. (`CLAIMS.patron` fragment — only if you use the full patron claim) |
| 1:10–end | End on Back this | **Back this.** |

## Tax deductibility

Mention **only** through `CLAIMS.grantFundDeductible`, and **only** if the gift is to the Sophia Fund / Abiding Practice path — not for backing a creative or project.

Hold that line until Park Hill's fiscal sponsorship is confirmed (Upsight). Default for this video: **omit deductibility.**

## Words to avoid

Same list as welcome: invest, donate (except fund path), gift-as-pitch for in-app backing, seed/water/harvest, platform/marketplace/solution/ecosystem as marketing fluff, public money / leaderboards, "your project will get funded."

## Casting / clearance

- One creative who said yes on camera and on the project page
- Seeded or real project with **Back this** live
- No other people's pledge amounts on screen
