# Welcome video — script

v1 · 2026-10-01 · for Rick's half-the-words pass  
Format: UI + captions explainer. Silent first. Voice and talking head later.  
Folder: `docs/marketing/creative-exchange/welcome-video/`

## Cuts (recommendation)

This is a how-to, not an ad.

- **Ship the full cut (~2:15).** That is the hero on Today and on the welcome page. People need the map.
- **Skip a 15-second Instagram vertical for this piece.** Fifteen seconds sells a feeling. It cannot teach five jobs.
- **Optional later: one ~60-second "start here"** — Post, Cheer / Back, monthly grant. Same captions, fewer beats. Use it for Patricia's welcome note or email, not for paid social.
- **Add chapter timestamps** on YouTube/Vimeo so someone can jump to Support or the monthly grant.

This draft is the full cut only. The 60-second path is marked under Optional short cut at the bottom.

## Length

~2:15 at speaking pace. Captions carry the mute case. Leave ~0.5s of black between beats.
