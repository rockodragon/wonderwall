Garden-first: the community is the shell

v1 · 2026-09-24 · exploration, not a decision · spec: docs/features/garden-first-ia.md · tokens: app/public/tokens.css

An alternate information architecture in which The Garden is the entry point and the shell, and creatives.exchange recedes to where the plan already puts it: receipts, account, and a footer line. Six screens: the current shell for comparison, the sidebar side by side, the signed-in home, visiting another community, the signed-out front door, and the phone.

Sample content only. Numbers, names and dates are placeholders in the register of the existing mocks and /demo; none are live figures.

S0Before — today’s shell, signed in

Reproduced from _app.tsx, CommunitySwitcher.tsx and search.tsx. The platform holds the wordmark; the switcher defaults to “All communities”; The Garden is a menu row; the first screen after sign-in is a people search. Nothing on the screen says you are in The Garden.

S1The sidebar, before and after

The smallest version of the change. The home community takes the wordmark slot, the platform moves to the foot of the rail (“receipts · account”), and the switcher becomes a short list of places you can visit. No new state: useCommunityContext keeps its ?community= param; the rail just stops presenting it as a lens.

S3Visiting another community

Other communities are places you go, not lenses you switch. The rail takes Abiding Practice’s name and gathering word (Cohorts) while you are there, a banner says whose place this is, and there is one way home. The visited community’s page is the existing /communities/:slug content; only the shell around it changes.
