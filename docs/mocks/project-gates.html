The project chooser & its gates

v1 · 2026-08-09 · spec: the-garden-product-plan.md §2.3, §3 · tokens: rebrand handoff

One fork, four gate states, two badges. The fork asks who brings the money — nothing else. Every denial is an honest sales moment: it names the locked value, says why now, shows the path, and leaves an exit. These gate screens carry the entire monetization model; they are where the product feels either honest or gross.

G0The fork — who brings the money?

Entry from “Start a project” anywhere. Two doors, no third. The distinction is stated in the user's own situation, never in taxonomy (“passion/paid” are our words; the cards speak theirs).

G6Denial anatomy — the contract every gate obeys

From the entitlements doc's paywall rules; G1–G3 are instances. Any new gate that can't fill all four lines shouldn't ship.

  1. Name the locked value. “Start your first project” — never “Upgrade required.”
  2. Say why now. “A seat runs one active project so every open project gets real attention.”
  3. Show the path. One citron button with the price on it. No “starting at,” no tiers table.
  4. Leave an exit. “Not now” / “Finish or archive current” — real alternatives, no nag, no shame.
