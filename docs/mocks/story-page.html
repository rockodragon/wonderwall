The public story page

v1 · 2026-08-09 · spec: product-plan §7 phase 0 · the page every other surface points at

The artifact of the whole system. Patrons forward it instead of writing reports; partners point at their credit on it; the cover module lives on it; the showcase quotes it. Work-led, credit-styled, public, and built to unfurl in a text message. One rule above all: the work leads — never the ask.

ST1The story page — a body of work with credits

Outsider view, no account. Hero is the newest work, full-bleed. Updates read as a reverse-chronological credit sheet: date · what happened · who made it possible. No counts anywhere (no views, no likes) — the deeds are the numbers. The cover module docks after the work has spoken, not before.

ST2The update composer — 90 seconds from cold

The loop only works if updates keep coming, so composing one must be nearly effortless: drop media, say one true thing, publish. The credit line attaches itself. A quiet nudge shows who's waiting — the patron relationship stated as warmth, not obligation.
